Our Story

Good clothes shouldn't cost the earth.

3FTERS started as a simple idea: the best pieces in your closet are rarely brand new. We hunt through streetwear, vintage, and everyday staples so you don't have to curating thrifted finds that are affordable, one-of-one, and easier on the planet than another fast-fashion order.

What we do

Every item we list is sourced, checked, and priced by hand no mystery bundles. We sort by style and gender so you can shop for exactly the look you're after, whether that's clean streetwear, campus wear, or everyday vintage staples.
